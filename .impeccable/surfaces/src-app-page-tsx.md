## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a 1:25 000 topographic sheet of Güllüdere (Rose Valley) and the Sunday run is the route drawn on it. Refuses the sports-club hero (black, action photo, red button) and the tourist brochure (balloons, sunset).

OWN-WORLD: Tuff-rose sheet ground, sienna contour lines with heavier index contours, umber ink, ultramarine route line that is also the only action color, dark umber "night sheet" bands. Photos sit as map insets inside neatline frames with tick marks and coordinate labels. Legend rows with drawn symbols replace cards. Archivo variable: wide heavy caps display, regular text, italic for place names. Square corners.

STORY: See the valley; learn it is every Sunday, open to every level, walking is fine, tea after; feel welcome; tap "Katıl".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: sheet collar header; left 5/12 slogan at ~6rem wide-heavy, one-line promise, ultramarine CTA plus Instagram link; right 7/12 Rose Valley photo inset in a neatline frame; contours fill the sheet behind; the route runs from the inset to a meeting-point pin. Mobile: photo inset first, slogan and CTA directly under; header always carries "Katıl".

SIGNATURE: the hero route draws itself once on load (stroke-dashoffset, expo ease-out), ending at the pin label. Reduced motion shows it drawn.

FORM: Topographic route sheet, #1 of my ordered list (Impeccable's pick, chosen by user); seed key e143c3d5.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
